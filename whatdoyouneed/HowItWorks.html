The Quick Version

Four steps from file to finished prints.

  1. Fill out the order form.
    Add your details, printing type, paper size, copies, and upload your file.
  2. Choose pickup or delivery.
    Pick your location, date, and preferred time. The admin confirms your schedule.
  3. Approve your quotation.
    We review your file and email you the final price. Printing starts after you approve.
  4. Receive your prints.
    Pick them up or wait for delivery. You can track your order anytime.
